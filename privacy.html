PriceBook - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-29 · PriceBook (com.ruicodex.pricebook) for Android · Controller: RuiCodex (Belgium) · info@ruicodex.com

This policy explains what data PriceBook handles, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

RuiCodex ("we", "us") is a company established in Belgium (European Union). RuiCodex is the controller within the meaning of Article 4(7) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") for the processing described in this policy concerning the Android app PriceBook (com.ruicodex.pricebook), except where a third party such as Google acts as an independent controller for its own processing, as explained below.

Because RuiCodex is established in the EU, the GDPR applies to this processing wherever you live. Our lead supervisory authority is the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données). Contact for all privacy matters: info@ruicodex.com.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up is needed, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Everything PriceBook stores (your settings, progress and anything you create in the app) stays on your device. We run no server that receives it.
  • The camera is used only to read barcodes, on your device. The optional product-name lookup (off by default) sends only the barcode number of a new product to Open Food Facts.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes some device data to deliver them, subject to your consent where required (see Advertising).
  • Optional purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Information stored on your device

PriceBook saves its data (for example settings, progress, history and content you enter) in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not transmitted to RuiCodex or to anyone else.

Apart from the data processed by the Google services described below, no personal data leaves your device.

You can delete this data at any time with the app's reset option (where available), with Android Settings > Apps > PriceBook > Storage > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include app data if you enabled it in your Google account; that backup is controlled by you and Google.

Camera (barcode scanning)

PriceBook asks for the camera permission only when you open the scanner, after explaining why. The camera image is analysed on your device (Google ML Kit barcode scanning, bundled in the app) to read the barcode number. No photo or video is stored, and no image leaves your device. You can use the app without the camera by typing the barcode or picking an item from the list.

Optional product-name lookup (Open Food Facts)

This feature is off by default. If you switch it on (when you scan a new barcode, or in Settings), the app sends only the barcode number of a newly scanned product to the free public database Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org, run by the French non-profit association Open Food Facts) to fill in the product name, brand and pack size. No prices, store names or identifiers are sent. As with any web request, Open Food Facts receives your IP address as part of the connection (Open Food Facts privacy policy). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by switching the lookup off. Everything else works offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland and by Google LLC elsewhere. To show, measure and protect ads, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address (country/city level; the app does not request location permission)
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version) and diagnostic data such as SDK performance
  • interactions with ads and the app (ad impressions, taps, app launches)

For personalised advertising and ad measurement, Google acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. For some functions (for example certain measurement and fraud-prevention services) Google may act as our processor under the Google Ads Data Processing Terms. We only receive aggregated, non-identifying reports from AdMob. Data is encrypted in transit.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform). Before any ad is requested, users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (UMP, based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). Personalised ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, Google may serve non-personalised or limited ads using minimal data, based on our legitimate interest in funding the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via "Privacy options" in the app's settings, without affecting earlier processing.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads). More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy, Google ad settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing under Google's terms and privacy policy; Google acts as an independent controller for payment processing. Your payment details and purchase history are held by Google; RuiCodex never receives your card or payment data.

To unlock what you bought, the app stores the product identifier and the purchase confirmation it receives from Google Play on your device (legal basis: performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Purchases can be restored on a new installation through Google Play with the same Google account. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy and your statutory consumer rights.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device by the app. They are not sent from a server and no data is transmitted for them. You can turn them off in the app or in Android Settings > Notifications.

Analytics

RuiCodex does not run its own analytics or crash-reporting service for this app and receives no usage data from it, apart from the aggregated advertising reports described above.

Usage counters and feedback

PriceBook counts a few events on your device (for example that the first price was logged) to decide when to show a tip or the store's review prompt. These counters never leave the device. If you use "Send feedback", your e-mail app opens with a pre-filled message to info@ruicodex.com containing your text, the app version and a few counts (never your prices or item names); nothing is sent unless you send the e-mail yourself.

Legal bases

  • Storing your data on your device so the app works: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); non-personalised ads, ad security and fraud prevention: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchases and restoring them: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligations such as accounting (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Answering your emails and requests: our legitimate interest in providing support (Art. 6(1)(f)) and, for rights requests, our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Who receives data

  • Google (AdMob advertising and the User Messaging Platform), as an independent controller or, for some functions, as our processor.
  • Google (Google Play billing), as an independent controller.
  • Our email provider, only when you write to us, as our processor.
  • Authorities and courts, only where we are legally required to disclose data.
  • Open Food Facts (only the barcode number, only if you switch the optional lookup on)

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with data brokers.

International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area. These transfers are based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) and, where Google is certified, on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

For users in Türkiye, cross-border transfers are carried out in accordance with Article 9 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

You can request information about these safeguards by writing to info@ruicodex.com.

Open Food Facts is hosted in the European Union; the optional lookup involves no transfer outside the EU/EEA.

Retention and deletion

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete it (reset, clear storage or uninstall); RuiCodex holds no copy.

Data collected by Google is retained by Google under its privacy policy; you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request, and at most three years.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (all users): access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection at any time to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), and withdrawal of consent at any time without affecting earlier processing (Art. 7(3)).

Complaints: you may lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données, dataprotectionauthority.be), our lead supervisory authority, or with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place (Art. 77 GDPR).

Users in Türkiye (KVKK, Art. 11): to learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; to learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties it is transferred to; to request correction, deletion or destruction and notification of these to third parties; to object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

United Kingdom and Switzerland: the equivalent rights under the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection. California and other U.S. states: to know, access, correct and delete personal information and to opt out of "sale", "sharing" or targeted advertising; we do not sell personal information for money. Other countries: the rights granted by your local law.

How: write to info@ruicodex.com. We answer within one month (Art. 12(3) GDPR; extendable by two further months for complex requests, in which case we tell you), free of charge. Because we do not ask for your identity and keep no copy of your app data, the data on your device is under your control and you can erase it yourself at any time.

Security

Data the app stores stays in Android's app-private storage. Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

PriceBook is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you are below the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country (13 in Belgium; up to 16 in some other EU countries, Art. 8 GDPR), use the app only with the consent of a parent or guardian, who should also approve any purchase. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact info@ruicodex.com and we will help delete it.

Ad requests are not tagged as child-directed and ad content is limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating.

Applicable law and disputes

This policy is governed by Belgian law and the GDPR. Where terms of use apply to PriceBook, they are also governed by Belgian law; if you are a consumer, this choice does not deprive you of the protection of the mandatory rules of the country where you habitually reside (Article 6 of the Rome I Regulation), and if you live in the EU you may bring proceedings in the courts of your country of residence, where we may also only sue you (Articles 17–19 of the Brussels Ia Regulation).

Out-of-court resolution: consumers in Belgium can submit a complaint free of charge to the Consumer Mediation Service (Consumentenombudsdienst / Service de Médiation pour le Consommateur, consumentenombudsdienst.be / mediationconsommateur.be). Consumers in other EU countries, Iceland and Norway can get free help with cross-border disputes from the European Consumer Centres Network (ECC-Net, eccnet.eu). Privacy complaints can always be made to a data protection authority as described above.
